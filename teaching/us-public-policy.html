The Politics of U.S. Public Policy

Course Description

This course focuses on public policy in the United States context. Birkland (2020: 248) states, "public policy is, in general, what the government, acting on our behalf, chooses to do or not to do." This course explores the policy process, including how problems are defined, the key actors involved in public policy, how agendas are set, why some issues gain more attention than others, differences in policy types, how decisions are made, and how policies are designed. In addition, it discusses key theories of the policy process and how policies are implemented and evaluated. This course aims to equip students with a set of tools and knowledge to evaluate and be an effective actor in the policy process.
